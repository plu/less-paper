Less Paper 3 is a complete rewrite.

• Rebuilt in SwiftUI, quicker to launch and to scroll
• The full filter set from the web interface, including custom fields
• Bulk edit, merge and delete across a selection
• Saved views can be created, edited and reordered in the app
• Import from the share sheet, password-protected PDFs included
• Client certificates, custom headers and reverse-proxy setups

Works with paperless-ngx 3.0.0 and earlier.
