Documents you can read with no server in reach, and a few things that were asked for.

• Favorites: keep chosen documents on the device — the PDF, its notes, metadata and custom fields — and read them offline. Pull to refresh and only the documents that actually changed are fetched again.
• Open a document straight from a lesspaper:// link
• A tip jar in Settings, for anyone who would like to support the app
• A tidier Settings screen

Works with paperless-ngx 3.0.0 and earlier.

If you hit a problem, please open an issue at https://github.com/plu/less-paper/issues — it is the quickest way to get it fixed.
