Share a document with anyone through a link — no Paperless account needed.

• Share link… is new in a document's Share menu, both in the list and in the viewer. It creates a public link to the document, the same share links the web UI offers: choose whether it expires after 1, 7 or 30 days or never, and whether to share the archived version. The link goes straight to the share sheet.
• The same sheet lists the document's existing links with when each expires. Tap one to share it again, or swipe to delete it.
• Share link… is offered only when your account may create share links and edit the document.

If you hit a problem, please open an issue at https://github.com/plu/less-paper/issues — it is the quickest way to get it fixed.
