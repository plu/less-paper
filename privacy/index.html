Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Johannes Plunien (“I”) develops and owns the Less Paper mobile application (the “Service”). The app collects no data about you. Nothing is shared with any third party, or with me, at any time.

This page explains my policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of personal data when you use the Service, and the choices you have in connection with that data.

Your documents

Less Paper talks directly to the paperless-ngx server you point it at. Your documents, and the credentials you use to reach that server, stay between your device and that server. They do not pass through me.

Information collection and use

I do not collect any personally identifiable information (“Personal Data”) from users of the app. I do not collect usage data or crash reports either. The app contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Legal basis for data processing

I do not collect or process any personal data through the app, so there is no processing by me that would require a legal basis under the GDPR. What happens on your own paperless-ngx server is under your control.

Changes to this privacy policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.

Supervisory authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority regarding the processing of your personal data.

Governing law

This privacy policy is governed by German law. Any disputes will be resolved in German courts.

Contact me

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact Johannes Plunien at johannes@plunien.com.
