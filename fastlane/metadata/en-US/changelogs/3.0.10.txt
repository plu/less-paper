Search finds documents again on the newest Paperless-ngx.

• Fixed: on Paperless-ngx 3.4 and later, search showed no results at all for any query that matched a document — not even the tags, correspondents or saved views it also matched. Newer servers send a shorter answer for documents in search, and the app now reads it. Older servers keep working as before.

If you hit a problem, please open an issue at https://github.com/plu/less-paper/issues — it is the quickest way to get it fixed.
