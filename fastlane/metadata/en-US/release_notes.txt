Less Paper 3.0.1 is a major rewrite — the app has been rebuilt from the ground up.

• Rebuilt in SwiftUI, quicker to launch and to scroll
• The full filter set from the web interface, including custom fields
• Bulk edit, merge and delete across a selection
• Saved views can be created, edited and reordered in the app
• Import from the share sheet, password-protected PDFs included
• Client certificates, custom headers and reverse-proxy setups
• Single sign-on with the provider your server is configured with
• Trash: restore a document you deleted, or empty it for good
• On iPad the document opens in a column beside the list
• A local error log you can read and share when something goes wrong

Works with paperless-ngx 3.0.0 and earlier.

Because so much has changed, something that worked for you before may not yet. If you hit a problem, please open an issue at https://github.com/plu/less-paper/issues — it is the quickest way to get it fixed.
