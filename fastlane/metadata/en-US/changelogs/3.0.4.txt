An app that follows your account's permissions, a smaller tip, and a diagnostic log that says more while revealing less.

• Controls your account has no permission to use are now hidden: editing, deleting, importing, bulk actions, notes, trash and the pickers in forms only appear when the server would actually allow them.
• Fixed: a restricted account could not add a server at all when it lacked permission to read users. The app now asks the server what you may do in a way every account is allowed to.
• The tip jar has a new smallest tip, and the tips are now sorted by price.
• A row in Settings for rating the app — and after a successful import or a tip, the app may occasionally ask for a review on its own. Apple limits how often.
• The diagnostic log no longer records server hostnames, and now notes the app version, connections and cache updates — so a shared log tells the story without telling on your server.

If you hit a problem, please open an issue at https://github.com/plu/less-paper/issues — it is the quickest way to get it fixed.
