Search now finds more than documents.

• Type in the search field on the Documents list and the matches appear grouped — documents, saved views, tags, correspondents, document types, storage paths and custom fields. Tap a document to open it, or tap anything else to filter the list by it.
• Choosing a result puts the keyboard away but keeps what you typed, so tapping the field again brings the same results back.
• Search terms are no longer written to the diagnostics log. That covers the advanced search too, which had been recording them since long before this release.

If you hit a problem, please open an issue at https://github.com/plu/less-paper/issues — it is the quickest way to get it fixed.
