Less Paper is a native iOS client for paperless-ngx, the self-hosted document archive. It talks directly to your own server, and to nothing else.

FIND ANYTHING
The complete filter set from the web interface, custom fields included: title and content, correspondent, document type, storage path, tags, dates and ASN. Save any filter as a view, then create, edit and reorder your saved views without leaving the app.

EDIT WHERE YOU ARE READING
Fix a title, reassign a correspondent, add tags, notes or custom field values from the document itself. Select several documents and change them together, merge them, or delete them in one go.

GET DOCUMENTS IN
Send anything on your device to paperless-ngx from the share sheet. Password-protected PDFs are unlocked on import, and the password is kept in the keychain so the next document from the same source opens without asking.

BUILT FOR REAL SETUPS
Connect as many servers as you like and switch between them. Client certificates, custom HTTP headers and reverse proxies such as Authelia are all supported.

KEEP THE ARCHIVE TIDY
Create, rename and delete tags, correspondents, document types and storage paths, and set ownership and permissions on any document.

Works with paperless-ngx 3.0.0 and earlier. Universal: iPhone and iPad.

Less Paper is open source. Issues and feature requests are welcome at https://github.com/plu/less-paper
